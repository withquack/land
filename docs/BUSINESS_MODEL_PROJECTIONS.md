- 20% of users purchase plugins monthly

**Year 1 Projections**:
- Month 1-3: 100 transactions/month × $10 × 15% = $150/month
- Month 4-6: 300 transactions/month × $10 × 15% = $450/month
- Month 7-9: 600 transactions/month × $10 × 15% = $900/month
- Month 10-12: 1,000 transactions/month × $10 × 15% = $1,500/month

**Year 1 Average**: $750/month additional MRR
**Year 3 Projection**: $5,000/month (as marketplace matures)

#### 2.2 Template Marketplace

**Pricing**:
- Free templates (80% of marketplace)

**Projections**:

- Year 2 End: 15 resellers = $4,500/month

**Revenue Share Bonus**: Additional 20% from reseller-managed servers
- Year 1: +$300/month
- Year 2: +$2,000/month
- Year 3: +$6,000/month

#### 2.4 Enterprise Solutions

**Features**:
- Dedicated account manager
- SLA guarantees
- Priority support
- Custom integrations
- Minimum: $500/month

**Projections**:
- Year 1: 10 enterprise clients = $5,000/month
- Year 2: 30 enterprise clients = $15,000/month

#### 2.5 API Access Tiers

**Pricing**:
- Free tier: 1,000 requests/day
- Developer: $19/month (10,000 requests/day)
- Business: $99/month (100,000 requests/day)
- Enterprise: $499/month (unlimited)

**Projections**:
- Year 1: 50 paid API users (avg $50/month) = $2,500/month
- Year 2: 200 paid API users = $10,000/month
- Year 3: 500 paid API users = $25,000/month

#### 2.6 Premium Add-ons

**Add-on Pricing**:
- Additional backups: $5/month (3 extra backups)
- Automatic modpack updates: $3/month
- Advanced DDoS (beyond standard): $10/month
- CDN for resource packs: $8/month
- Multi-server management: $15/month

**Attach Rate Assumptions**:
- 30% of customers purchase at least one add-on
- Average add-on revenue: $8/customer/month

**Projections**:
- Year 1: 500 servers × 30% × $8 = $1,200/month
- Year 2: 2,000 servers × 30% × $8 = $4,800/month
- Year 3: 5,000 servers × 30% × $8 = $12,000/month

#### 2.7 Creator Partnership Program

**Model**:
- Free servers for YouTubers/Streamers with 10k+ subscribers
- Revenue share: QuackHost gets affiliate fee for referred customers
- Affiliate commission: 20% of first 6 months revenue

**Projections**:
- Year 1: 20 creators, avg 5 referrals/month = 100 new customers
  - 100 × $20 × 20% × 6 months = $2,400 (one-time over 6 months)
  - Recurring: $400/month after ramp
- Year 2: 100 creators, 500 new customers = $10,000/month
- Year 3: 300 creators, 1,500 new customers = $30,000/month

## Churn Reduction Strategy

### Current Churn: 15% monthly

### Improvements via New Features:

1. **Improved Onboarding** (Templates, 1-click modpacks)
   - Reduces early churn by 40%
   - Impact: -2% churn

2. **Better Support** (AI assistant, knowledge base)
   - Reduces support-related churn by 30%
   - Impact: -1.5% churn

3. **Lock-in Effects** (Multi-server networks, integrations)
   - Increases switching costs
   - Impact: -2% churn

4. **Premium Features** (Marketplace purchases, custom configs)
   - Engaged users churn less
   - Impact: -1.5% churn

5. **Community Building** (Discord, forums)
   - Social connections reduce churn
   - Impact: -1% churn

**Target Churn Rate**: 7% monthly (by Year 2)

**Impact on LTV**:
- Old LTV: $20 / 0.15 = $133
- New LTV: $25 (higher ARPU) / 0.07 = $357
- **LTV Increase**: 168%
