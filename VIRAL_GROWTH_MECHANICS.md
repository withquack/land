# QuackHost Viral Growth Mechanics
## Engineering Growth Into Every Feature

---

## 🎯 Core Principle

**Every feature should answer: "How does this help us grow?"**

**Target:** Achieve K-factor >1.5 (sustainable viral growth)

K-factor formula: `K = (invites per user) × (conversion rate)`
- Target: 5 invites × 30% conversion = K-factor of 1.5
- Result: Exponential growth without paid acquisition

---

## 🔄 The Viral Loop

### Primary Loop: Free Tier Branding

```
User creates free server
    ↓
10 friends join to play
    ↓
See "Powered by QuackHost" everywhere
    ↓
2 friends click through (20% CTR)
    ↓
1 friend signs up (50% conversion)
    ↓
Creates their own server
    ↓
[Loop repeats]
```

**Math:**
- 1 free server = 10 players exposed
- 20% click = 2 clicks
- 50% signup = 1 new user
- **K-factor = 1.0** (self-sustaining growth)

**Optimization Goals:**
- Increase players per server (10 → 15)
- Increase CTR (20% → 25%)
- Increase conversion (50% → 60%)
- **New K-factor = 2.25** (explosive growth)

## 💰 Viral Economics

### Cost-Benefit Analysis

**Referral Program:**
- Cost: $10 credit per conversion
- Value: $240 LTV (12 months × $20)
- ROI: 24:1
- Plus: Referrer becomes more engaged (higher retention)

**Free Tier Virality:**
- Cost: $1.26/month per free user
- Viral signups: 1 per free user per year
- Conversion: 10% × $240 LTV = $24
- ROI: 19:1

**Creator Program:**
- Cost: 15% commission ($3/month per customer)
- CAC savings: $50 vs $10 (80% reduction)
- Net benefit: $40 per customer

**Total Viral CAC: $5 (vs $50 paid)**
**Payback: <1 month (vs 3+ months paid)**
