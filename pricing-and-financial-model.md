## Usage-Based Pricing (Beta)

### Pay-Per-Player-Hour Model

**Problem**: Fixed plans waste money (paying for 40 slots when 10 are used)

**Solution**: Only pay for active player time

```
Pricing:
├── $0.02 per player-hour
├── Example: 20 players for 5 hours = $2.00
├── Monthly cap: Same as equivalent tier
└── Autoscale: Resources adjust automatically

Comparison:
Traditional PRO ($19/mo):
├── 40 player slots
├── 720 hours/month (30 days)
└── Total capacity: 28,800 player-hours

Usage-based:
├── Actual usage: 500 player-hours
├── Cost: $10 (47% savings!)
├── Or: 2,000 player-hours = $19 (same price)
└── Cap at $19 (never pay more than Pro tier)

Benefits:
✅ Save money during low-traffic times
✅ Burst capacity during events
✅ Pay only for what you use
✅ No waste

Launch Timeline:
├── Q2 2024: Beta testing
├── Q3 2024: Public launch
└── Q4 2024: 30% of customers on usage-based
```

## Unit Economics Deep Dive

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Channels & Costs**:
```
Organic (40% of customers):
├── SEO: $0 CAC
├── Word-of-mouth: $0 CAC
└── Community: $10 CAC (forum hosting, events)

Paid (60% of customers):
├── Google Ads: $80 CAC
├── Reddit Ads: $50 CAC
├── YouTube Sponsorships: $60 CAC
└── Affiliate Program: $40 CAC (40% commission on first month)

Blended CAC: $60 (Year 1) → $78 (Year 5)
Why increasing? Targeting higher-value customers (enterprise)
```

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Calculation**:
```
LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin × Customer Lifetime (months)

Year 1:
├── ARPU: $25/mo
├── Gross Margin: 60%
├── Avg Lifetime: 20 months (5% monthly churn)
└── LTV: $25 × 0.60 × 20 = $300

Year 5:
├── ARPU: $65/mo
├── Gross Margin: 80%
├── Avg Lifetime: 50 months (2% monthly churn)
└── LTV: $65 × 0.80 × 50 = $2,600

LTV Growth Drivers:
✅ Higher ARPU (upsells, add-ons)
✅ Better margins (scale efficiency)
✅ Lower churn (better product, support)
```

### Cohort Analysis

**Retention by Cohort**:
```
Month 1 cohort (1,000 customers):
├── Month 1: 1,000 (100%)
├── Month 3: 850 (85%)
├── Month 6: 700 (70%)
├── Month 12: 550 (55%)
├── Month 24: 400 (40%)
└── Month 36: 300 (30%)

Target Retention:
├── Month 1 → 3: 90% (reduce early churn)
├── Month 6 → 12: 80%
└── Month 24+: 60% (core customers)

Strategies:
- Onboarding improvements (reduce M1-M3 churn)
- Quarterly check-ins (proactive support)
- Loyalty program (discounts for long-term customers)
```

## Pricing Experiments & A/B Tests

### Test 1: Anchor Pricing
```
Control:
├── Starter: $9
├── Pro: $19
├── Business: $49

Test (add "Ultimate" tier):
├── Starter: $9
├── Pro: $19
├── Business: $49
└── Ultimate: $99 (makes Business look cheap)

Hypothesis: More people choose Business (anchor effect)
Result: +15% conversion to Business tier ✅
```

### Test 2: Freemium
```
Control: No free tier

Test: Free tier with limits
├── 1GB RAM
├── 5 players
├── Ads on control panel
├── Limited hours (4 hours/day)

Hypothesis: Free users convert to paid
Result:
├── 10% of free users upgrade within 30 days ✅
├── CAC reduced by 30% (free tier is top of funnel) ✅
└── Support costs increased 20% (free users need help) ⚠️

Decision: Keep freemium, limit support (community-only)
```

### Test 3: Annual Billing Discount
```
Control: Monthly only

Test: Annual option
├── Monthly: $19/mo
├── Annual: $180/year (save $48, 21% off)

Result:
├── 35% choose annual ✅
├── Reduced churn (committed for 12 months) ✅
├── Cash flow boost (upfront payment) ✅
└── LTV increase: +25%

Decision: Offer 20% annual discount permanently
```
