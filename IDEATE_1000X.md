## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

### Key Risks

1. **Execution Complexity**
   - *Risk:* Too ambitious, can't deliver
   - *Mitigation:* Phased rollout, MVP testing, hire aggressively

2. **Market Education**
   - *Risk:* Users don't understand revenue share model
   - *Mitigation:* Clear messaging, case studies, freemium entry

3. **Competition**
   - *Risk:* Established players copy features
   - *Mitigation:* Network effects, AI moat, community lock-in

4. **Technical Debt**
   - *Risk:* Moving too fast creates instability
   - *Mitigation:* Invest in DevOps, testing, monitoring from day 1

5. **Regulatory**
   - *Risk:* Payment processing, COPPA, GDPR complexity
   - *Mitigation:* Legal team, partner with compliant payment processors
