## 12. 💰 Financial Projections & Unit Economics

### 12.1 Revenue Model Deep Dive

**Average Revenue Per Server (ARPS)**
```
Freemium (40% of servers):     $0/mo
Starter (35% of servers):      $12/mo
Growth (20% of servers):       $45/mo
Pro (4% of servers):           $120/mo
Enterprise (1% of servers):    $800/mo

Weighted ARPS = (0.4×0) + (0.35×12) + (0.2×45) + (0.04×120) + (0.01×800)
              = $0 + $4.20 + $9.00 + $4.80 + $8.00
              = $26/server/month
```

### 16.2 Technical Failure Modes

**Scenario 1: Database Corruption**
- Detection: Automated integrity checks every hour
- Response: Restore from PITR backup (5-minute granularity)
- Prevention: Multi-region replication, checksums

**Scenario 2: Complete Region Outage**
- Detection: Health checks, multi-region monitoring
- Response: Automatic failover to secondary region (< 60s)
- Prevention: Active-active multi-region setup (Year 2)

**Scenario 3: Billing System Fails**
- Detection: Transaction monitoring, reconciliation
- Response: Manual billing, customer credits
- Prevention: Idempotent billing, extensive testing

### 16.3 Business Risks

**Market Risk**: Gaming shifts away from Minecraft
- **Hedge**: Multi-game strategy from Year 1
- **Signal**: Monitor Minecraft player trends monthly

**Competitive Risk**: Price war with Shockbyte/Apex
- **Defense**: Differentiate on quality, not price
- **Alternative**: Introduce value-brand tier

**Execution Risk**: Ship too slow, lose momentum
- **Counter**: Weekly shipping cadence, small batches
- **Metric**: Track feature velocity, lead time

## 20. 🎓 Learning & Iteration Strategy

### 20.1 Validation Framework

**Before Building ANYTHING, Validate**
1. **Problem**: Do customers actually have this pain? (10 interviews)
2. **Solution**: Will they pay for this? (landing page test, 5% conversion)
3. **Product**: Can we build it? (technical spike, 2 days)
4. **Business**: Is it profitable? (unit economics model)

**Metrics for Validation**
- Problem: 7/10 interviewees say "I'd pay for this"
- Solution: 100+ email signups in 1 week
- Product: MVP in <2 weeks
- Business: LTV:CAC > 3x

### 20.2 Experimentation Rhythm

**Weekly Cycle**
- Monday: Review metrics, set hypothesis
- Tuesday-Thursday: Ship experiment
- Friday: Analyze results, iterate or kill
- Weekend: Customer interviews (if needed)

**Example Experiments**
- Week 1: Test pricing ($10 vs $15 vs $20) → $15 wins
- Week 2: Onboarding flow (3 steps vs 1 step) → 1 step wins
- Week 3: AI assistant CTA placement → Top-right wins
- Week 4: Free trial (7 days vs 14 days) → 7 days wins

### 20.3 Customer Feedback Loops

**Continuous Feedback**
- NPS survey: Monthly (target >50)
- In-app feedback: Always visible
- Discord community: Daily interaction
- 1-on-1 interviews: 5 per week
- Support tickets: Tag + analyze themes

**Act on Feedback Fast**
- Bug reports: Fix within 24 hours
- Feature requests: Respond within 48 hours (not build, but acknowledge)
- Complaints: Founder personally responds to all
