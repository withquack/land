## 📊 Program Tiers

### Tier 1: Affiliate 🥉
**Requirements:**
- 1,000+ subscribers/followers on any platform
- Active gaming content (uploaded in last 30 days)
- Email list OR Discord server (optional)

**Benefits:**
- **15% recurring commission** on all referrals (lifetime)
- Custom referral code (e.g., `YOURNAME`)
- Free QuackGrow server ($10/month value)
- Affiliate dashboard with real-time analytics
- Co-branded landing page (`quackhost.gg/YOURNAME`)
- Marketing assets (banners, overlays, scripts)
- Monthly payout (minimum $50 threshold)

**Expectations:**
- Mention QuackHost in 1+ video/post per month
- Use affiliate link in descriptions
- Honest, authentic recommendations

---

### Tier 2: Partner 🥈
**Requirements:**
- 10,000+ subscribers/followers
- OR 25+ referral conversions in 90 days
- Consistent content schedule

**Benefits:**
- **20% recurring commission** on all referrals
- Free QuackPro server ($25/month value)
- Early access to new features (beta testing)
- Dedicated partner manager (email support)
- Featured on QuackHost homepage (monthly rotation)
- Custom server template showcase
- Quarterly performance bonus (top 100 partners get $100-$1000)
- Priority payouts (bi-weekly, $25 minimum)

**Expectations:**
- Mention QuackHost in 2+ videos/posts per month
- Join partner Discord for collaboration
- Feedback on new features

---

### Tier 3: Elite ⭐
**Requirements:**
- 100,000+ subscribers/followers
- OR 500+ referral conversions total
- High-quality, family-friendly content

**Benefits:**
- **25% recurring commission** + performance bonuses

- Dedicated server support engineer
- Featured in QuackHost advertising campaigns
- Co-created content (joint videos, blog posts)
- Speaking opportunities at QuackCon
- Custom integrations (API access for unique features)
- Exclusive Elite Discord channel
- Weekly payouts (no minimum)
- Revenue share on merchandise (if applicable)

**Expectations:**
- Major campaign participation (product launches)
- Monthly check-in calls with partner manager
- Collaborative content planning
