## 5. Unit Economics

**Assumptions:**
- Average customer lifespan: 36 months
- Average ARPU: $25 (blended across tiers)
- Gross margin: 75%

**Calculation:**
```
LTV = ARPU × Avg Lifespan × Gross Margin
LTV = $25 × 36 × 0.75
LTV = $675
```

## 9. Financial Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Slower Growth
**Impact:** Miss revenue targets by 30-50%
**Mitigation:**
- Multiple growth channels (paid, organic, viral)
- Freemium model ensures volume
- Marketplace provides additional revenue

### Risk 2: Higher Churn
**Impact:** LTV drops 40%, need to raise more capital
**Mitigation:**
- Product excellence (NPS >70)
- Marketplace lock-in
- Community building
- Proactive retention programs

### Risk 3: Price Compression
**Impact:** ARPU drops 30%, margins shrink
**Mitigation:**
- Differentiate on value, not price
- Premium features (AI, marketplace)
- Enterprise tier insulates from price wars

### Risk 4: Infrastructure Costs
**Impact:** Gross margin drops to 65% (vs 75%)
**Mitigation:**
- Bulk purchasing
- Spot instances
- Auto-scaling efficiency
- Negotiate enterprise deals with cloud providers

### Risk 5: Competition
**Impact:** CAC increases 2-3x
**Mitigation:**
- Build moats early (marketplace, data, community)
- Move fast, dominate before competitors catch up
- Category creation vs competition
