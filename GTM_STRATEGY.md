# QuackHost Go-To-Market Strategy

**Last Updated:** 2025-11-16
**Focus:** Year 1 Execution Plan

---

## Executive Summary

QuackHost's GTM strategy focuses on community-led growth, leveraging a freemium model to acquire users virally while converting high-value customers through superior product and marketplace monetization. Target: 121K servers and $1.75M ARR by end of Year 1.

**Core Strategy:**
1. **Freemium** to drive massive adoption (viral loop)
2. **Community-led growth** (creators evangelize for us)
3. **Content dominance** (SEO + YouTube + education)
4. **Premium positioning** (best product justifies higher price)

## 2. Pricing & Packaging Strategy

### 2.1 Freemium Model

**Free Tier: "Hatchling"**
- **Price:** $0/month
- **Limits:** 5 players, 1GB RAM, 5GB storage
- **Purpose:** Viral acquisition, word-of-mouth
- **Conversion Strategy:**
  - After 7 days, prompt upgrade ("Server full? Upgrade for $5")
  - Show comparison: "97% of servers with >10 players upgrade"
  - Email drip campaign highlighting paid features

**Why This Works:**
- **Low barrier to entry:** Anyone can try
- **Viral:** Free users invite friends → more users
- **Upsell momentum:** As community grows, natural upgrade
- **Competitive:** Better than ScalaCube's free tier

### 2.2 Paid Tiers

| Tier | Price | Players | RAM | Storage | Target % |
|------|-------|---------|-----|---------|----------|
| **Duckling** | $5/mo | 10 | 2GB | 10GB | 25% |
| **Quacker** | $15/mo | 25 | 4GB | 25GB | 20% |
| **Mallard** | $30/mo | 50 | 8GB | 50GB | 10% |
| **Goose** | $60/mo | 100 | 16GB | 100GB | 4% |
| **Swan** | $120/mo | Unlimited | 32GB | 200GB | 0.9% |

### 2.3 Add-Ons (Revenue Boosters)

- **Extra Storage:** $5/mo per 25GB
- **Priority Support:** $10/mo (30min response SLA)
- **Dedicated IP:** $5/mo
- **Extra Backups:** $3/mo (hourly vs daily)
- **Custom Domain:** $2/mo (server.yourname.com)

**Revenue Impact:** +15% ARPU

### 2.4 Annual Discounts

- **Annual:** 2 months free (16.7% discount)
- **Biennial:** 4 months free (20% discount)

**Benefits:**
- Improved cash flow
- Lower churn (committed longer)
- LTV boost

**Target:** 30% of paid customers on annual by EOY1

## 4. Conversion Optimization

### 4.1 Landing Page Strategy

**Homepage:**
- Clear value prop above fold
- Social proof (reviews, testimonials)
- Trust signals (uptime badge, security)
- CTA: "Start Free Server"

**Pricing Page:**
- Simple tier comparison
- Highlight most popular (Quacker)
- Annual discount visible
- FAQ section

**Game-Specific Pages:**
- "Minecraft Server Hosting"
- "Rust Server Hosting"
- Tailored messaging per game
- Game-specific features highlighted

### 4.2 Signup Flow Optimization

**Step 1: Game Selection**
- Visual game selector (not dropdown)
- Popular games first
- Search for less common games

**Step 2: Plan Selection**
- Default to free tier
- Show what each tier unlocks
- Estimated player count per plan

**Step 3: Server Configuration**
- Pre-filled with smart defaults
- "Quick Start" vs "Advanced" mode
- Version selection (latest by default)

**Step 4: Account Creation**
- Social login (Google, Discord, GitHub)
- Minimal fields (email, password)
- No credit card for free tier

**Step 5: Server Deployment**
- Progress bar (exciting!)
- "Your server is launching..." (2 min)
- Tips while waiting
- Celebration when done

**Conversion Rate Target:**
- Landing → Signup: 15%
- Signup → Server Created: 90%
- Free → Paid (within 30 days): 15%

### 4.3 Onboarding Optimization

**Day 1:**
- Welcome email with quick start guide
- First login tour (5 steps)
- Prompt to invite friends
- Success checklist

**Day 3:**
- "How's your server going?" email
- Offer help (live chat CTA)
- Highlight unused features

**Day 7:**
- Upgrade prompt if server is full
- Case study (similar server that upgraded)
- Limited-time discount (10% off first month)

**Day 14:**
- Survey: "What would make you upgrade?"
- Feature education (marketplace preview)
- Community invitation

**Day 30:**
- "You're still on free tier!" email
- Show what you're missing
- Social proof (join 10K paid servers)

### 4.4 A/B Testing Roadmap

**Month 1-2:**
- Headline variations (5 tests)
- CTA button color/text (3 tests)
- Free tier limits (test 5 vs 10 players)

**Month 3-4:**
- Pricing display (monthly vs annual default)
- Social proof placement
- Signup flow steps (3 vs 5 steps)

**Month 5-6:**
- Onboarding email cadence
- Upgrade prompts timing
- Free trial length (forever vs 30 days)

**Month 7-12:**
- Feature bundling
- Upsell messaging
- Referral incentive amounts

## 5. Retention Strategy

### 5.1 Churn Prevention

**At-Risk Indicators:**
- Haven't logged in 7+ days
- Server offline >48 hours
- No backups taken
- Support tickets unresolved
- Payment failures

**Interventions:**
- Automated email: "We miss you!"
- Offer to help (live chat)
- Pause subscription option (vs cancel)
- Win-back discount (50% off next month)

**Target Churn:** <8% monthly (Year 1), <3% (Year 3)

### 5.2 Engagement Programs

**Monthly Challenges:**
- "Server of the Month" contest
- Featured on homepage
- Free upgrade for 3 months
- Interview + case study

**Community Events:**
- Virtual meetups (Discord)
- QuackCon (annual conference, Year 2+)
- Build competitions
- Plugin development hackathons

**Education:**
- QuackUniversity (free courses)
- Certification program
- Monthly webinars
- Office hours with team

### 5.3 NPS & Feedback

**NPS Survey:**
- Triggered after 30 days
- "How likely to recommend?" (0-10)
- Follow-up: "Why did you give us X?"
- Act on feedback quickly

**Target NPS:**
- Year 1: 50 (good)
- Year 2: 60 (great)
- Year 3: 70+ (world-class)

**Feedback Loop:**
- Public roadmap (vote on features)
- Monthly "You Asked, We Built" updates
- Close the loop (email when feature ships)
